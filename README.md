# unplugged_card_game-young_enterprise
Unplugged is a 100% recyclable card game tackling teen screen time through real-world fun. Roll the dice to answer colour-coded trivia—correct answers earn pieces to build your plug, while wrong answers draw unpredictable wild cards. First team to complete their plug wins!
## 🔍 Monthly Secret Treasure Hunt
Think you have sharp puzzle skills? Every month, we hide a **brand-new secret treasure hunt** somewhere on our website! 

Visit [unplugged-cardgame.lovable.app](https://lovable.app), keep your eyes peeled for hidden clues in plain sight, and see if you can crack this month's puzzle.
